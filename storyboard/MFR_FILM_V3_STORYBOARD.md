- **Technical Spec:** `1920 × 1080` (`16:9`), `24.000 fps` (`4,032` frames), `Rec.709`, `-14.0 LUFS` Stereo AAC (`mfr_master_mix_168s_v2.m4a`)
- **Visual Rules:**
  1. Every 3D robot geometry uses **only** verified `MFR.step` (`1,370` nodes, `363,001` tris, `224.0 × 220.2 × 274.6 mm`).
  2. All suspended vertical-glass CGI carries **`CONCEPTUAL FACADE OPERATION — NOT FIELD VALIDATED`**.
  3. All presenter segments (`P1–P4`) use clean filming placeholder slates until real team camera footage is captured (zero AI-generated fake human faces).
